PLP Pitch Deck · 01

SmartFarm Kenya

Connecting farmers to customers

A digital marketplace designed to help Kenyan farmers sell fresh produce directly to buyers.

Founder: Your Name

02 · The Problem

Farmers need better access to markets.

  • Farmers may struggle to find reliable buyers.
  • Middlemen can reduce farmers' earnings.
  • Fresh produce can go to waste when sales are delayed.
  • Customers need dependable sources of fresh food.
03 · Our Solution

A simpler way to sell fresh produce.

  • Connect farmers directly with potential buyers.
  • Display available produce and prices online.
  • Allow buyers to place orders conveniently.
  • Help farmers reach more customers.
04 · Product

Meet the SmartFarm marketplace.

Farmers create listings for vegetables, fruit, grains and other produce. Buyers browse listings, contact sellers and arrange delivery or collection.

First version: a simple, mobile-friendly website.

05 · Target Customers

Who will use SmartFarm?

  • Small and medium-sized farmers.
  • Households buying fresh food.
  • Restaurants and hotels.
  • Retail shops and produce traders.

Initial focus: selected farming communities and buyers in Kenya.

06 · Market Opportunity

A large everyday need.

Food is essential, and farmers need dependable ways to reach customers. Smartphone access and digital payments create opportunities for online agricultural marketplaces.

First step: Interview local farmers and buyers to measure demand and identify the best launch area.

Market size figures still need research.

07 · Competition

Why would customers choose us?

  • Traditional brokers and open-air markets.
  • Existing produce traders and delivery services.
  • Other online marketplaces.

Our proposed advantage: local farmer relationships, clear listings and convenient communication.

We will validate these advantages through customer interviews.

08 · Business Model

How we could earn revenue.

  • A small commission on completed orders.
  • Optional delivery coordination fees.
  • Future paid services for frequent business buyers.

Pricing will be tested with farmers and buyers before launch.

09 · Marketing Strategy

Start locally. Grow through trust.

  • Partner with farmer groups and cooperatives.
  • Demonstrate the service at local markets.
  • Use WhatsApp and social media to reach customers.
  • Offer early users a simple onboarding experience.
  • Encourage referrals from satisfied customers.
10 · Milestones

Our first 90 days.

  • Month 1: Interview farmers and buyers; validate the problem.
  • Month 2: Build a basic marketplace prototype.
  • Month 3: Pilot with a small group and collect feedback.

These are proposed goals, not completed achievements.

11 · Financial Plan

Test the numbers before scaling.

  • Potential costs: website development, marketing, support and operations.
  • Potential revenue: commissions and optional services.
  • Track orders, average order value, costs and repeat customers.

Budget, pricing, sales forecasts and funding needs are to be researched.
